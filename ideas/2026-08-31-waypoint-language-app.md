## The Core Idea

**Waypoint** — honest milestones on your language journey. Finite phases with clear exit criteria, not endless Duolingo grinding. Korean first, expanding to Japanese, German, and more.

## Why Now?

Adult learner wants structured language learning. App built as MVP, moved to dedicated repo for incremental multi-language development.

## What was built

- Phase-based curriculum (Hangul → Foundation → Immersion → Conversation)
- Hangul quiz, drama phrase miner, Anki export
- PWA for mobile install
- Weekly routine tracker
