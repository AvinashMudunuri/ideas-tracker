## The Core Idea

A language learning app with finite phases and clear exit criteria — not endless Duolingo grinding. Korean first (motivated by K-drama), expanding to Japanese, German, and more.

## Why Now?

Adult learner (40) wants structured Korean learning. App built as MVP, then moved to dedicated repo for incremental multi-language development.

## What was built

- Phase-based curriculum (Hangul → Foundation → Immersion → Conversation)
- Hangul quiz, drama phrase miner, Anki export
- PWA for mobile install
- Weekly routine tracker
