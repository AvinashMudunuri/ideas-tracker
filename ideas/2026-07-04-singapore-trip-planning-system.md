# Singapore Trip Planning System

**Date:** 2026-07-04  
**Status:** Raw Brain Dump

---

## The Core Idea

Build a comprehensive trip planning system to track all aspects of a family trip to Singapore: prerequisites (visas, vaccinations, bookings), mandatory items (documents, packing), expenses, and split costs among travelers.

---

## Why Now?

Planning a family trip to Singapore with mixed ages (4 adults, teen boy 12, baby girl 4) requires coordinating many moving parts. Need a single place to track everything so nothing falls through the cracks.

---

## Initial Thoughts

**Trip Details:**
- Destination: Singapore
- Travelers: 4 adults + 1 teen (12 years) + 1 baby girl (4 years)
- Need to coordinate with brother's family

**Core Features Needed:**
- **Prerequisites tracker**
  - Passports (check expiry dates)
  - Visas/entry requirements
  - Vaccinations/health requirements
  - Travel insurance
  - Flight bookings
  - Hotel reservations
  - Activities pre-booking

- **Mandatory Items Checklist**
  - Documents (physical + digital backups)
  - Baby essentials (diapers, formula, stroller)
  - Teen entertainment/devices
  - Medications
  - Adapters/chargers
  - Emergency contacts

- **Expense Tracking**
  - Flights
  - Accommodation
  - Food/dining
  - Activities/attractions
  - Transportation
  - Shopping
  - Emergency fund
  - Total budget per category

- **Split-wise Integration**
  - Split costs between two families
  - Track who paid what
  - Calculate balances
  - Different split ratios (maybe adults vs kids pricing)

**Technical Considerations:**
- Could be a simple spreadsheet to start
- Or a web app for real-time updates
- Mobile-friendly for on-the-go access
- Offline capability?
- Shared access for both families

**Age-specific Needs:**
- Baby (4): nap schedules, kid-friendly restaurants, stroller accessibility
- Teen (12): engaging activities, wifi access, age-appropriate attractions
- Adults: balance relaxation with exploration
